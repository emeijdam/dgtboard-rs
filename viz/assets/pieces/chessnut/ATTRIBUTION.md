"chessnut" by [Alexis Luengas](https://github.com/LexLuengas), as shipped in
[lichess-org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila) at
`public/piece/chessnut/`. Licensed
[Apache 2.0](https://github.com/LexLuengas/chessnut-pieces/blob/master/LICENSE.txt).

Files: the twelve `{w,b}{K,Q,R,B,N,P}.png` here are the published SVGs
rasterised unmodified at 256 × 256 px with `rsvg-convert`; the SVGs
themselves are not included.
