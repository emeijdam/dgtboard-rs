dgtboard 3D

A live DGT chess board rendered in real time with Bevy + WebGL2 — a photoscanned marble set (CC0, Poly Haven), PBR lighting, shadows and bloom, or a flat Lichess-style board (chessnut pieces, Apache-2.0) when you'd rather read the position. Both show the same overlay: last move, the reply you're expected to play, check, resync hints, a ghost of the opponent's piece, and clocks. The whole thing is driven by the same dgtboard-core Rust crate, compiled to WebAssembly.
Click “Connect board”, then pick the DGT serial port.
3D: left-drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Keys: Tab 2D/3D, F side. Chromium browsers only (Web Serial isn't in Safari/Firefox). Demo overlay
