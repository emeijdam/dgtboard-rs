dgtboard 3D

A live DGT chess board rendered in real time with Bevy + WebGL2 — a photoscanned marble set (CC0, Poly Haven), PBR lighting, shadows and bloom. The whole scene is driven by the same dgtboard-core Rust crate, compiled to WebAssembly.
Click “Connect board”, then pick the DGT serial port.
Left-drag to orbit · scroll to zoom. Chromium browsers only (Web Serial isn't in Safari/Firefox).
